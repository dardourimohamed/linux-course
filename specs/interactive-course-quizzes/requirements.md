# Requirements Clarification

This document records the Q&A process for refining the interactive quiz feature.

---

## Q1: What approach should we use for implementing quizzes?

**Answer:** Use `mdbook-quiz` plugin

---

## Q2: What types of quiz questions should be included?

**Answer:** Multiple choice questions

---

## Q3: Should quiz answers include explanations?

**Answer:** Yes, answers include explanations

---

## Q4: Which chapters should have quizzes?

**Answer:** All chapters (13 chapters across 4 parts)

---

## Q5: How many quiz questions per chapter?

**Answer:** 5-10 questions per chapter (varies by complexity)

---

## Q6: Where should quizzes appear?

**Answer:** At the END of each chapter in a dedicated section (changed from natural break points)

---

## Q7: What difficulty level?

**Answer:** Playful challenging

---

## Q8: Should there be immediate feedback?

**Answer:** Show explanation after answering and allow multiple attempts

---
